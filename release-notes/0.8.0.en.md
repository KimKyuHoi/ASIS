## New

- **GIF Studio** — Pick any frame of a recorded GIF and annotate it with text, shapes, blur, and more. It also includes a viewer for `.gif` files and a video-to-GIF converter.
- **Image Studio** — Open images you already have (PNG, JPG, HEIC, TIFF, BMP, WebP) and mark them up in the editor. Open it from "Image Studio…" in the menu bar or with "Open With" in Finder. Your original file is never changed.
- **Crop in the editor** — Keep just the part of a capture you need. Shapes you drew stay in place, and cropping can be undone.
- **Edit again** — Reopen any of your 10 most recent captures with [Edit] in Capture History and keep editing the shapes you drew.
- **Show input while recording** — GIF and screen recordings can show where you click and the shortcuts you press (⌘/⌃ combinations). Turn it on in Settings. Regular typing is never shown.
- **New scrolling capture** — Choose up or down when you start and ASIS scrolls and captures for you, or scroll yourself and it keeps capturing. Scrolling upward stitches in the right order, the captured area is outlined, and you're told if any part couldn't be stitched.
- After a GIF recording, choose to polish it in the studio, save it right away, or copy it to the clipboard.
- Step guides can be exported as a single HTML file you can share right away. Edit each step's caption, annotate steps with the editor tools, and discard a recording with [Cancel] on the recording pill.
- Light and dark mode, with a new design.
- English UI. Pick your language on first launch.
- Apply a left-hand shortcut preset in one click, and turn off shortcuts you don't use.
- The first time you launch a new version, a short message appears once. Click "View Release Notes" to open these notes.

## Improved

- With two displays, choose whether a full-screen capture takes the display under the cursor or all displays.
- Select an area on any display. ([PR #9](https://github.com/KimKyuHoi/ASIS/pull/9))
- The area being recorded (GIF or screen) is outlined, and the outline disappears as soon as you press Stop.
- During full-screen video recording, the recording pill now stays visible on screen (it is not recorded).
- Choose what happens after a capture: open the editor or copy right away.
- Opening another image while you're editing no longer throws away your work — it opens after you finish, in order.
- The editor window's yellow (minimize) button now hides the window; bring it back with "Show Hidden Windows" in the menu bar.
- The menu bar menu now lists every feature and shows at the top when a recording is in progress.
- It's easier to tell whether a pin is locked (click-through).
- If a file with the same name already exists in the save folder, ASIS adds `-2`, `-3` instead of overwriting it.

## Fixed

- Fixed an empty window showing up in Mission Control. ([PR #8](https://github.com/KimKyuHoi/ASIS/pull/8))
- The editor window can now be moved by dragging its title bar. ([PR #6](https://github.com/KimKyuHoi/ASIS/pull/6))
- Fixed area selection picking up "Window Server" as a window while the macOS screenshot tool was open.

## Removed

- Time Machine and OCR (text recognition) have been removed.
